Our Trail

From small towns to big cities, coastlines to mountainsides, every place along our trail has been an adventure to say the least. Traveling the world around us and living among vastly different ladscapes has taught us a bit about that place we like to call home. We have sifted for shells along the Florida coast, camped under an Arizonan night sky even hiked through the Bavarian hillsides, and yet nothing could ever match or keep us away from the Rocky Mountain landscape. Our goal was simple, keep exploring this magical landscape we call home, share it with everyone who feels that same way and help protect it for generations to come. There truly is no place like home!
